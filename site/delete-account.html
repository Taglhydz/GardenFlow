Delete your GardenFlow account

From the app

  1. Open GardenFlow and log in.
  2. Tap your picture or your initial at the top of the home screen to open your profile.
  3. At the bottom of the profile, tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password (none for an account created with Google), then tap Delete for good.

The account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Send an email to contact@gardenflow.fr from the address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". The address is used to check that the request comes from the owner of the account. Your account is deleted within one month at most, and you receive a confirmation by email.

What is deleted

  • your account: username, email address, password, birthdate, link with your Google account;
  • your profile picture;
  • all your gardens, parcels, zones, crops, comments and plant colors.
The deletion is permanent: nothing is kept in the app. The (encrypted) backup copies that still contain this data are erased automatically after 30 days at most.

More details in the privacy policy.
