Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains which data the GardenFlow app collects, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how to exercise your rights.

In short: GardenFlow only collects what makes your account and your gardens work. No advertising, no usage statistics, no location, and your data is neither sold nor shared.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Tom Vaillant, private individual, developer of GardenFlow. For any question or request: contact@gardenflow.fr.

2. Data collected

DataDetails
Your account Username, email address, password (stored only as an irreversible encrypted hash, never in clear), birthdate (optional), role of the account.
Sign-in with Google If you use it: the identifier of your Google account, your email address and your name (only used to suggest your username). GardenFlow never receives your Google password.
Profile picture Optional: the photo you choose (resized by the app before it is sent), or a plant avatar.
Your gardens What you enter: names, place (optional, free text), descriptions, plans of the parcels and zones, soil types, crops (plants, dates, comments), colors chosen for the plants.
Technical data Your IP address is used for 15 minutes at most, in memory, to limit repeated login attempts; it is not stored. The server keeps a technical log of the requests (date, address called, result), without IP address or password, erased as it goes; your email address only appears in it when an email could not be sent to you.
On your phone Your login token (in the secure storage of the phone) and your preferences (last garden opened, plan settings, hidden help). They are erased when you log out or uninstall the app.

GardenFlow does not access your location, your contacts or your other files. Your photos are only accessed when you choose a profile picture.

3. Why this data is used

  • Running the service: creating and managing your account, saving your gardens, computing the plant suggestions and your gardener level. Legal basis: providing the service you ask for by creating an account.
  • Security: verifying your email address, limiting login attempts, allowing password resets. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in protecting the accounts.
  • Emails: only emails about your account (address verification, address change, forgotten password). No newsletter, no advertising.

4. Where your data is and who can access it

Your data is stored on a server located in France. Only the developer of GardenFlow can access it. It is not sold, rented or shared. A few service providers help run the service, without being able to use the data for their own purposes:

  • Cloudflare (secure connection between the app and the server): sees the encrypted traffic and your IP address;
  • OVHcloud (France): sending the emails and hosting this website;
  • Google: if you use Sign-in with Google; and storage of the backup copies, encrypted before they are sent (Google cannot read them).

Cloudflare and Google are US companies: data may be transferred outside the European Union, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework they take part in.

5. How long it is kept

  • Your account and your gardens: as long as your account exists.
  • When the account is deleted: everything is erased immediately (account, picture, gardens, crops). The encrypted backup copies that still contain it are erased after 30 days at most.
  • The links sent by email expire: 1 hour for a forgotten password, 24 hours for the address verification.

6. Your rights

At any time you can access your data, correct it, delete it, ask for a copy (portability), object to its use or ask for its restriction.

  • Correct: directly in your profile (name, email address, password, picture) and in your gardens.
  • Delete: in the app or by email, see Delete your account.
  • Any other request: write to contact@gardenflow.fr from the address of your account. You will get an answer within one month.

If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or the authority of your country.

7. Security

The traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as an irreversible hash (bcrypt). The links sent by email work only once and expire. You can log out all your devices from your profile; changing your password logs them out too.

8. Children

If you are under 15, ask one of your parents for permission before creating an account.

9. Changes

This policy may change with the app. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page; in case of an important change, you will be told in the app or by email.
